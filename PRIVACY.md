# Privacy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

In short: **what you make stays in your browser, and you aren't tracked.**

## No tracking

There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels and no fingerprinting.
No personal data is collected, sold or shared.

## What you make stays in your browser

Memes and backgrounds are drawn in your browser, on your device:

- images you upload, drop or paste are read by the page in your browser, and never sent anywhere;
- the texts you write, and your design, are never sent anywhere;
- “Download” and “Copy” make the picture on your device.

The meme or background you are working on is kept in the address bar, after the `#` (uploaded
images aren't). Browsers never send that part of the address to the website. It only leaves your
device if you share the link yourself: then whoever opens it sees your texts.

## Stored on your device

Two viewer preferences (whether the alternatives panel is open, and whether duplicates are shown)
are saved in your browser's local storage, so they're remembered on your next visit. They never
leave your device. Clearing the website's data in your browser removes them.

## Requests your browser makes

- **This website**: your browser downloads the page, the list of templates and their images from
  it. Like any web host, its hosting provider may keep standard technical logs of these requests
  (IP address, address requested, time, browser) for security and operation. They aren't used to
  track you.
- **mermaid.ink**: the pictures in the Diagrams category are loaded from
  [mermaid.ink](https://mermaid.ink), which then sees your IP address. They are this website's own
  example diagrams: nothing you make is sent there.
- **Links**: “View on imgflip”, “View on Know Your Meme”, “Edit GIF on imgflip” and “Edit in
  mermaid.live” open other websites, only when you click them. Their own privacy policies apply
  there.
