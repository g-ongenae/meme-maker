# Privacy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

In short: **what you make stays in your browser, and this website doesn't track you.**

## No tracking

The website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels and no
fingerprinting. It collects no personal data of its own, and doesn't sell or share any.

## What you make stays in your browser

Memes and backgrounds are drawn in your browser, on your device:

- images you upload, drop or paste are read by the page in your browser, and never sent anywhere;
- the texts you write are never sent anywhere;
- “Download” saves the picture as a file on your device, and “Copy” puts it in your clipboard.

## What the address bar keeps

- **Your search, category and sort order** are kept in the address, after the `?` (for example
  `?q=drake&category=gifs`), so a link brings you back to the same list. Your browser sends this part
  to the website when it loads the page, so **what you search for can appear in the host's logs**
  (see below).
- **A meme made from a template, or a background**, is kept after the `#`: its texts, their style,
  the settings, and the templates and characters of this website put in it. Images you upload,
  drop or paste aren't, and neither is a meme made from an image of your own. Browsers never send
  the part after the `#` to the website.

Like any address, these links are kept in your browser's history and bookmarks (and synced, if
your browser syncs them), and whoever you share one with sees what's in it.

## Stored on your device

Two viewer preferences (whether the alternatives panel is open, and whether duplicates are shown)
are saved in your browser's local storage, so they're remembered on your next visit. They never
leave your device. Clearing the website's data in your browser removes them.

## Requests your browser makes

- **This website**: your browser downloads the page, its scripts, styles and fonts, the lists of
  templates and characters, and their images and videos from it. Like any web host, its hosting
  provider may keep standard technical logs of these requests (IP address, address requested,
  including the search after the `?`, time, browser) for security and operation. This website
  doesn't use them to track you.
- **mermaid.ink**: the pictures of the diagrams are loaded from [mermaid.ink](https://mermaid.ink),
  as they come into view (in the Diagrams category, and in “All templates”), when one is opened,
  and when one is downloaded. mermaid.ink then sees your IP address and the diagram requested: one
  of this website's own examples, never anything you make.
- **Links** open other websites only when you click them, and their own privacy policies apply
  there: “View on imgflip”, “Edit GIF on imgflip”, “View on Know Your Meme” (which searches for the
  template's name), “Open original” and “Edit in mermaid.live” for diagrams, and the Terms, Privacy
  and Source links at the bottom of the page (on GitHub).
